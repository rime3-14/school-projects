# Formal verification of double-word arithmetic

**4A research internship · LIP, ENS de Lyon**

During my research internship, I used Gappa to analyze error bounds for numerical algorithms that represent a value as the sum of two floating-point numbers. I translated assumptions and intermediate steps into proof scripts for addition, division and square root, then documented how the solver established bounds on rounding errors.

I verified that representative scripts run with Gappa 1.8.0. The proof scripts and derivations are stored in a private research repository.
