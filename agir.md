# Introducing computing through Scratch

**INSA Lyon · 4A semester 2 · AGIR team project**

We designed a computing workshop for middle-school students that combined algorithms, interface design and communication. The project addressed stereotypes about computing careers by making programming a team activity where students could build and present something of their own.

The workshop used Scratch and produced student projects in Villefranche. Our coursework also outlined how teachers could run the activity in a technology class.
