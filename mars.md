# MARS Banking Dashboard

**INSA Lyon · 4A semester 1 · Systems architecture team project**

The team designed a fictional banking network around service continuity at branch offices. The architecture study examined partitioning customer and account records by branch and replicating product and contract definitions from headquarters. It considered the consistency trade-offs of serving a branch during a connectivity outage.

The React dashboard prototype illustrates staff workflows for contact details, customer relationships, subscriptions and the contract catalog. It uses mock data and local interactions. The source export builds with Vite and is stored in a private repository.
