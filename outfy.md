# OUTFY | Wardrobe assistant concept

**INSA Lyon · 5A · User Interface Design · Team project**

The design tackles two points of friction in wardrobe apps. Cataloging every garment makes setup time-consuming, and browsing a catalog still leaves the morning outfit decision to the user.

We proposed using photos people already take to start building a digital wardrobe. The concept then presents three outfit choices based on the day's weather, with an option to swap a single garment. The pitch included a mock-up of the outfit cards and a roadmap from the core interaction prototype to garment recognition and calendar context. The coursework deliverable is a design concept.
